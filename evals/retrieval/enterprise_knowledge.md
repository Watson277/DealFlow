# DealFlow Enterprise Capability Guide

## Identity and Access Management

DealFlow supports SAML 2.0 and OpenID Connect single sign-on. SCIM 2.0 automated user provisioning is available on the Enterprise plan. Administrators can enforce multi-factor authentication and role-based access control.

## Audit and Compliance

The platform records immutable administrator and user audit events. Audit logs are retained online for 180 days and can be exported to a customer SIEM. DealFlow maintains ISO 27001 certification and provides annual SOC 2 Type II reports.

## Availability and Disaster Recovery

The Enterprise service-level agreement guarantees 99.95% monthly availability. The documented disaster-recovery targets are a four-hour recovery time objective and a one-hour recovery point objective.

## Deployment and Integration

The managed service exposes REST APIs and outbound webhooks. A dedicated on-premises deployment requires custom engineering and is not part of the standard SaaS subscription.

## Data Residency

Customers can select data residency in mainland China or Frankfurt. Cross-region replication remains within the selected legal region.

## Support Plans

Enterprise support operates around the clock, including weekends and public holidays. Priority-one incidents have a 30-minute initial response target.
